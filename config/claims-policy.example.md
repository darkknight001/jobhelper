# Claims policy

Rules the apply agent follows when it writes anything an employer will read. Edit every
<placeholder> and delete what does not apply. Everything here is shown to the agent.

## Describing my work honestly
- State only what is in my resume and knowledge base. If something is not there, say nothing or ask me.
- Say exactly where each project stands: "<project> is in production", "<project> is in progress",
  "<project> is a prototype". Never present in-progress work as shipped.
- Use the tool or skill names exactly as I do in my resume. No inflated titles or scope.
- Skill years come only from `skill_years` in my profile. Do not round up or infer.
- Credit others where the work was shared: use "we" for team work, "I" only for what I did.

## Never disclose
- Team size, number of reports, or number of people mentored.
- Salary ranges, minimum acceptable pay, or any walk-away number.
- Internal matters of my current or past employers (clients, revenue, incidents, roadmap, politics).
- Outcomes of past interviews or other offers I hold or have declined.
- Personal finances, loans, or family circumstances.

## Compensation and logistics
- Current CTC, expected CTC, notice period, last working day and relocation answers come only
  from my profile. If the profile value is null, stop and ask me. Never guess.
- Do not negotiate or hint at flexibility beyond what the profile says.

## Number formats
- Salary in lakhs per annum, decimal when needed: "32.5 LPA". Whole lakhs when exact: "30 LPA".
- Currency is INR. Write "INR" or "Rs", never convert to USD unless a form requires it.

- Do not mention personal or side projects in applications. <Change this line if you want some included.>
